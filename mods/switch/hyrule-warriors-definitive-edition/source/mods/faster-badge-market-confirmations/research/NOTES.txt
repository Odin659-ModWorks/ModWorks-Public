Target: Hyrule Warriors Definitive Edition 1.0.1, BID 815A2C19D1767896

FUN_71003034b4 identifies the currently selected badge tile and invokes
FUN_7100301954 after a badge is created. FUN_7100301954 starts the badge_make
animation and the corresponding attack, defense, or support result effect.

The five patched instructions load the animation playback rate for the creation
and result effects. Each original instruction is `fmov s0, #1.0`; each patch is
`fmov s0, #4.0`. This preserves the animations and surrounding state changes
while reducing their duration to one quarter.

The replacement instruction was assembled and verified in Ghidra as word
0x1E221000. The original word 0x1E2E1000 was restored after verification.
