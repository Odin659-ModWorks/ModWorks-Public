Target: Hyrule Warriors Definitive Edition 1.0.1, BID 815A2C19D1767896

FUN_710036ee30 updates the blacksmith top-level result flow.
When the completion flag at object +0x41364 is set, it waits for three subordinate states
and two result-animation collections before clearing that flag and presenting message 0x1ee.

The two calls at 0x710036efbc and 0x710036efcc query whether those animation collections
have completed. This patch replaces only those calls with `mov w0, #1`. It preserves all
subordinate-state checks, the result message, game-state changes, sounds, and other UI flows.
