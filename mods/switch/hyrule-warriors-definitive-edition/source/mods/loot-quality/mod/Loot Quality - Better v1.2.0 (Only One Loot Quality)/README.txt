Loot Quality - Better v1.2.0
Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition 1.0.1
Build ID: 815A2C19D1767896

Raises the floor while preserving the ceiling:
- Random weapon rank is selected from the upper half of the eligible rank table.
- Every newly created weapon has at least 3 stars; natural 4- and 5-star rolls remain possible.
- Randomly generated weapons have a skill-slot budget of at least 6; natural 7- and 8-slot rolls remain possible.

The upper-half rank range also makes the highest eligible rank more likely. The shared star floor covers fixed scenario-reward weapons, while their guaranteed identity/rank and curated skills remain unchanged.

Enable only one Loot Quality variant at a time.
