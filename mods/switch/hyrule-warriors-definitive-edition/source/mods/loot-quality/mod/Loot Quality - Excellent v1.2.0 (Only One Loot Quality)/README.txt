Raises the floor while preserving the ceiling:
- Random weapon rank is selected from the upper quarter of the eligible rank table.
- Every newly created weapon has at least 4 stars; natural 5-star rolls remain possible.
- Randomly generated weapons have a skill-slot budget of at least 7; natural 8-slot rolls remain possible.

The upper-quarter rank range also makes the highest eligible rank substantially more likely. The shared star floor covers fixed scenario-reward weapons, while their guaranteed identity/rank and curated skills remain unchanged.

Enable only one Loot Quality variant at a time.
